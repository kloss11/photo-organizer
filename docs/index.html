PhotoOrganizer

Organize your photos & videos by the date they were taken — safely.

Turn a messy pile of photos and videos into tidy Year / Month / Day folders. Everything runs on your computer — nothing is uploaded.

Made to be trusted with your memories

It moves your irreplaceable photos — so it's built to be careful, transparent and reversible.

🛡️

Safe by design

Always preview the full plan first — nothing moves until you say so. And you can undo the last run at any time.

🔒

Completely private

Runs 100% locally. No cloud, no upload, no account. Your files never leave your computer.

🎯

Smart date detection

Reads the real capture date from EXIF, video metadata, file dates — even dates hidden in file names.

🖥️

Works everywhere

One app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Self-contained — no installer, no .NET to set up.

🌍

Speaks your language

Polski, English, Deutsch, Русский, Español, Français — switch instantly in the app.

☁️

Cloud-aware

Skips OneDrive/cloud "online-only" placeholders and duplicates, so it never forces downloads or makes a mess.

Three simple steps

No surprises. You're always in control.

1

Preview

Point it at a folder and see exactly what would move, and where — a full dry-run. Nothing is touched.

2

Apply

Happy with the plan? Apply it, and your files land in clean YYYY/MM/DD folders.

3

Undo

Changed your mind? One click restores everything to exactly where it was.
